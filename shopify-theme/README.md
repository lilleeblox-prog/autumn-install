# Palette Install — Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme

**Transition status:** This is a work-in-progress Shopify adaptation, **not yet the approved Website's full-screen 50/50 builder**. Its homepage starts with palette and scale, then package product pages finish the design and approve it into Shopify's native cart. It also includes gallery, studio, process, FAQ, contact, collection, and cart pages. Keep the current Shopify site live; do not publish this ZIP as its replacement until the full Website design and ordering flow are prepared and approved. Text, images, sections, menus, colors, and footer content are editable in Shopify's theme editor; package details and paid services are Shopify products.

## Upload and preview

1. Upload the supplied `palette-install-shopify-theme.zip` in Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload zip file. The archive has the `assets`, `config`, `layout`, `sections`, and `templates` folders at its root.
2. **Preview and customize the unpublished theme first.** Do not publish until package prices, products, photos, service area, installation policies, and checkout settings have been reviewed.
3. This theme cannot be installed automatically through the current Shopify connection: it has `read_themes` but not `write_themes`. No Shopify theme has been changed or published.

## Configure the unpublished theme

1. In Customize → Home page → Package Browser, choose The Stoop, The Porch, and The Estate Shopify products in the matching blocks. Their expandable contents lists are editable as one item per line in each package block; keep them aligned with the package facts on the Website and in Shopify Admin. The Porch is preselected; keep **Preselect this scale** enabled on only one package block if you change the default. Until each product is active, published to Online Store, and priced, its scale card cannot continue to the product page. The first section on the homepage is the builder at `#compose`.
2. In Customize → Home page → Package Browser, open each **Palette study image** block. The three supplied palette photographs are already bundled as landscape studies, including the requested Classic Harvest orientation. Adjust **Horizontal image position**, **Vertical image position**, and **Image zoom for repositioning** independently for each palette. **Rotate image 180°** flips it if needed; an uploaded Image overrides its bundled study. Keep each block's Palette and built-in photo matched. The Gallery page and product page also have independent image-position controls in their image blocks, using portrait copies.
3. In Customize → Products → Default product → Product Information, verify the six size-specific **delivery** and **removal** service product settings and the **vinyl artwork** product setting. The local product template references the prepared draft handles; confirm they resolve to the intended products after activation. The theme charges one delivery line for either fulfillment choice, plus removal and vinyl lines only when selected. Installation setup is free; The Porch and The Estate automatically carry the included pair of hay bales as a line-item property, without a separate paid product or customer toggle. A selected paid service without an available matching product and approved price cannot be approved for ordering. Add optional Upgrade Product blocks only for finishing touches you actually offer and have priced.
4. Create Shopify pages with handles `about` (title: Studio), `gallery`, `process`, `faq`, and `contact` in Online Store → Pages. Assign their matching theme templates (`page.about`, `page.gallery`, `page.process`, `page.faq`, `page.contact`). The theme cannot create those pages merely by having templates. Add them to your main navigation and link Compose to the home page's `#compose` section. Avoid a generic All Products link if it would expose paid service products as standalone displays.
5. Confirm Shopify's native contact form delivers inquiries to the correct store email. Review the service area, installation policies, shipping, taxes, payment setup, customer-facing copy, and checkout behavior before publishing.

**After the Shopify move:** Once the new theme and Gallery page are in place, remind the merchant to install and connect [Mintt Instafeed](https://apps.shopify.com/instafeed) to the professional Instagram account. Add the app's feed section to the Gallery template on the unpublished theme and verify real posts appear before relying on it. Keep selected-palette study photographs curated and accurately labeled; do not assign unreviewed Instagram posts to palettes. This feed is not a prerequisite for uploading or previewing the theme, and it does not automatically update the separate Replit Website preview.

When everything is approved, switch products from Draft to Active, make them available on the **Online Store** sales channel, then publish the theme. A product must be active and available on that sales channel to appear in the theme.

The contact form uses Shopify's native contact form. Palette and scale selections on the homepage, and selected product-page details, carry into the consultation form in the shopper's current browser tab for up to two hours. The shopper can edit the prefilled text and add their own contact details; artwork file bytes are never transferred. The separate Website preview shows an editable consultation draft but does not send inquiries. The cart and checkout use Shopify's native cart and checkout.

## Customer approval and cart editing

On the Shopify homepage, a customer chooses a palette and a scale, then continues to that package's product page with the palette retained. There they choose delivery or installation, optional services, requested week, and notes. For vinyl-wrapped pumpkins they upload a PNG or PDF (up to 20 MB), choose a white or orange pumpkin and white, black, or gold vinyl, and set the quantity. **Approve design** submits the artwork through Shopify's native multipart product form. When Shopify redirects to the cart, the theme checks that the file reached the line item, adds the grouped paid services, and reloads the cart. An interrupted service addition blocks the theme's checkout button rather than blindly retrying a potentially chargeable line. Changes to any choice clear approval. Approval is not an order or date reservation. An unpriced package cannot be approved. A request for further customization goes to the Shopify-native contact form and cannot be ordered without consultation.

On the cart page, each line and its price are visible. **Edit design** on the base display reopens the original choices; approving the changes adds the updated group before removing the old one. The artwork file must be uploaded again on edit. **Remove composition** removes its base display and related services together. Grouped line quantities are intentionally locked: use Edit design to change services or vinyl artwork counts so the cart cannot accidentally charge for a mismatched composition. Standalone items can still be removed or have their quantities updated. The cart checks grouped lines and the artwork URL before its checkout button can be used. JavaScript is required for this grouped ordering journey. This theme-level check is not a bypass-proof checkout rule: **add Shopify-side validation for artwork and paid-service quantities before making the artwork service available for public checkout**. Test a real PNG and PDF upload in an unpublished Shopify theme before launch; local Node tests cannot verify Shopify's file hosting behavior.

These theme changes are in the zip for manual upload; they are **not yet installed in Shopify**. The current Replit web preview is a separate design reference and does not share Shopify's online-store cart session. Do not send customers to that preview as an ordering site. None of these drafts should be activated for ordering until the merchant approves the offers and prices, updates the palette variants, and finishes Shopify store setup. Keep the old theme published until the new one has been previewed and approved.
